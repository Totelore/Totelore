Behind the Tale

Where passion meets sustainable artistry

Welcome to Totelore! Totelore began with two medical students, a love for painting, and a dream to create something of our own. What started as a simple idea soon became a collection of hand-painted canvas totes, each telling a unique story...

Every piece is carefully painted by hand, making no two totes exactly alike. Thoughtfully crafted, sustainably made, and designed to carry more than just your essentials because, we believe EVERY TOTE HAS A TALE.

How to Order

Simple steps to get your favorite tote home

1

Via Instagram App

Click "Order on Instagram" under your chosen tote → You will see the corresponding post on Instagram app → just tap "View Product" to place your order!

2

Without Instagram App

Simply send us an email with the tote name and your location!
